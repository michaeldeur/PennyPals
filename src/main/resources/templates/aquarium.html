Aquarium Quick-Start Guide

Welcome to your interactive virtual aquarium! Here is how you can customize and manage your tank:

  • Earn Reward Pennies: Log transactions, maintain your budget, and complete daily quests to earn pennies.
  • Shop for Items: Visit the Shop page to purchase unique fish species, flora, and intricate decor items.
  • Live Swimming Engine: Click anywhere on the water to feed pellets. Equipped items populate your canvas tank in real-time.
